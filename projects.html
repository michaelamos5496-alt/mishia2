In schoolsWith Orange Sanitary Pads

Sanitary pads for girls

In collaboration with Orange Sanitary Pads, we provide sanitary pads to schools, so young girls have access to essential hygiene products.

On 27 May 2024, Mi Shia Foundation marked World Menstrual Hygiene Day at Osu Presbyterian Secondary School. Over 500 female students took part under the theme “menstrual friendly world”, joined a Q&A session, and all were given packs of sanitary pads.

Partner
Orange Sanitary Pads
Reach at one event
Over 500 students
What we gave
Sanitary pads for every student, and education on menstrual hygiene
