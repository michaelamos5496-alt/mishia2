About us

We believe charity starts at home.

Mi Shia is a nonprofit charity founded in 2019. “Mi Shia” means “my home” in Ga, and our mission is to help Ghana, our home, in any way possible.

We reach out to rural areas where basic necessities are hard to come by. We are small but growing, and we strive to do as much as we can, as often as we can.

Our vision

Free medical aid for rural villages.

We aim to extend our impact by providing free medical aid to these villages. We aspire to establish simple healthcare services and small clinics, bringing essential medical care closer to those who need it most.

With your help, we hope to achieve this in the near future, creating healthier and more resilient communities.

The founder

Dr. Djifa van Staalduinen

After earning her medical degree in 2021, she chose to channel her energy into a different kind of healing: she founded Mi Shia Foundation.

Dr. Djifa van Staalduinen is a dynamic and passionate young woman ready to take the world by storm and bring others along for the ride. Driven by a lifelong desire to uplift those in need, she founded the Mi Shia Foundation.

Growing up, Djifa was deeply affected by the hardships faced by those with fewer resources. Frustrated by the inefficiencies of other organizations, she decided to create a foundation where every effort directly benefits those in need. While she views this work as a heartfelt hobby, Djifa’s enthusiasm and dedication are infectious.

Through the Mi Shia Foundation, she hopes to inspire others to tag along and join her mission to make the world a better place, one compassionate act at a time.

What does “Mi Shia” mean?

In Ga, “Mi Shia” means “my home”. We chose it because we believe charity starts at home. Our slogan is “This is our home”.

What does Mi Shia do?

We are a nonprofit founded in 2019. We hand out food, clothing, stationery and toiletries to rural communities in Ghana, including Ayim Village and Gonteng Village, and we work with partners such as Orange Sanitary Pads to provide sanitary pads to schools.

What is the long-term goal?

Free medical aid for rural villages and, in time, small clinics. Our upcoming medical and dental health screening is a step in that direction.

How can I support the foundation?

You can give, partner with us or fund a project, or help us raise awareness. Use the Support Us button or contact us at info@mishiafoundation.org and we will reply with the details.

Can my organisation partner with Mi Shia?

Yes. One of our objectives is collaborating with local and international partners to expand our reach and resources. Choose “Partnership or grant” in the contact form.

Who founded Mi Shia?

Dr. Djifa van Staalduinen, a medical doctor since 2021, founded the foundation in 2019.

Orange Sanitary Pads collaborates with Mi Shia Foundation to promote Menstrual Hygiene

Mi Shia Foundation has partnered with Orange Tulip to celebrate the World Menstrual Hygiene Day on Monday May 27, 2024 at the Osu Presbyterian Secondary School.

Mi Shia Foundation has partnered with Orange Tulip to celebrate the World Menstrual Hygiene Day on Monday May 27, 2024 at the Osu Presbyterian Secondary School.

The event which took place at the Osu Presbyterian Church Chapel, gathered over 500 female students with the aim to educate young women on menstrual hygiene under the theme “menstrual friendly world.”

According to the team, this collaborative effort aimed to empower young women with knowledge and resources to manage their menstrual health effectively.

Speaking at the event, founder of Mishia Foundation, Miss Dzifa renowned for her presence on TikTok and a qualified medical doctor, hinted at the significance of maintaining personal hygiene, particularly during menstruation.

Mr. Michael Eshun, Chief Operations Officer at Orange Sanitary, emphasized that periods are natural and not a cause for shame or disgust.

The students actively participated in a Q&A session, seeking clarification and sharing insights. Some students received gifts, while all were provided with packs of sanitary pads courtesy of Mishia Foundation and Orange Charities, the corporate social responsibility arm of Orange Sanitary Pads.
